01 / Data Engineering

Data Engineering Pipeline on Oracle Cloud

Team project with Michael Harpole

An end-to-end pipeline on Oracle Cloud. Raw data lands in Object Storage, an ETL instance in a private subnet loads it into Autonomous Data Warehouse, and OCI Data Science notebooks query the clean data for analysis.

  • Designed a VCN with public and private subnets so Object Storage, the ETL instance and the warehouse are not exposed to the internet.
  • Wrote Python with the OCI SDK to read from Object Storage through a private endpoint and connect to ADW using its wallet.
  • Ran the analysis in OCI notebooks against ADW, covering product profitability, customer lifetime value, product affinity, retention and sales forecasting.

03 / Security

Cyber Literacy & OCI Security

Hardening the PubChem app against the OWASP Top 10

I revisited the PubChem app and addressed the most relevant OWASP vulnerabilities. Then I redeployed it on OCI with tighter access controls and a load balancer.

  • Blocked cross-site scripting in the search field with input sanitization and a Content Security Policy.
  • Prevented SQL injection with parameterized queries, input validation and an ORM for authentication.
  • Fixed broken access control by enforcing authentication and storing passwords as hashes.
  • Tightened OCI IAM policies and added a load-balanced deployment.

04 / Data Science

Auto MPG Exploration

MIT Applied Data Science Program

I explored 398 cars from 1970 to 1982 and used PCA and t-SNE to cut seven engineering features down to a handful of components. That revealed three distinct groups of vehicles by efficiency, weight and age.

  • Cleaned the data by treating six ? horsepower values as missing, imputing them, and dropping the high-cardinality car name column.
  • Found that MPG has strong negative correlations with cylinders, displacement, horsepower and weight (about −0.8 for each).
  • The first three principal components explain about 90% of the variance, which reduces 7 features to 3.
  • t-SNE separated the cars into three clusters: heavier, older, low-MPG cars; newer mid-range cars; and the lightest, most efficient cars.

05 / Data Analysis

Global Emissions Analysis

Kaggle global methane emissions dataset

An exploratory analysis of methane emissions by region, segment, type and sector, to understand where emissions come from and what drives them.

  • Asia Pacific (led by China), North America (led by the U.S.), and Russia and the Caspian produce the most emissions.
  • Onshore oil is the single largest emissions segment.
  • Energy accounts for 54% of total emissions, followed by agriculture at 29.4% and waste at 14.6%.

06 / Data Analysis

FIFA World Cup Data Analysis

MIT Applied Data Science Program

An analysis of every World Cup from 1930 to 2014 for a fictional new club, covering goals, attendance, host cities, winners, and whether playing at home makes a difference.

  • Home teams won 57% of matches, compared with 22% draws and 20% away wins.
  • The best-attended match was Uruguay vs. Brazil in 1950, with 173,850 people.
  • Mexico City hosted the most matches (23). Brazil has the most titles, with 5.
  • Total goals per tournament rose over time, peaking in 1998 and 2014.

07 / Data Analysis

Spotify Top 100 Songs

Kaggle most streamed songs dataset

An exploratory look at Spotify's 100 most-streamed songs: who made the list, who dominates streams, and how audio features like energy, valence and duration are distributed.

Maternal Mortality Dashboard

Tableau Public

A Tableau dashboard comparing maternal mortality across developed nations, and breaking down U.S. rates from 2018 to 2021 by race and ethnicity and by age.

  • A treemap shows the U.S. has the highest maternal mortality rate among the developed nations compared.
