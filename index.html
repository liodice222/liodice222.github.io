About me

Data Engineering | Cloud Technologies | Environmental and Biological Science

As a Data Engineer, I have written Python and SQL transformations, designed staging and validation layers, and built automated data quality checks. Most recently, I led validation for a migration of 10 million records from IBM DB2 to Snowflake.

I spent two years at Deloitte provisioning Oracle Cloud environments with Terraform and CI/CD, building automation scripts with Python, and creating reporting dashboards, which taught me how the infrastructure under a data platform works. I have leaned on that experience when working with cloud providers like OCI, AWS and GCP.

I hold a B.S. in Biology, and I'm especially interested in where environmental and health sciences meet data. I do my best work on a collaborative team that turns business requirements into reliable data products.
